What works

Your own sessions

A first-run how-to, four presets as starting points, then your numbers. Saved in your browser; export and import as JSON.

A live halftone load map

Each lobe grows with that foot's share of the load and pulses on contact. Hover to read it.

A runner on your angles

Drawn in code: knee fold, hip swing, bounce and cadence from your session, real motion blur, a motion-capture skeleton.

A protocol from your data

Findings, left/right symmetry and a three-phase plan aimed at the side your numbers point to. Copy, download, print.

The designer's look

Transitional serif, halftone textures, the black View Protocol button, drifting dot blobs: faithful to the reel.

Phones and reduced motion

Stacks on phones; a single still frame when motion is reduced.

Not yet

  • No sensors or video: you type the numbers in from a gait lab, a watch or a coach.
  • The protocol is rules of thumb from your numbers, not a clinician. Midstance is not a medical device.
  • No cloud sync or session comparison view yet (duplicate a session to compare).
